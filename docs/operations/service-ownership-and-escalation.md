# Service ownership and escalation

Repository catalog ownership is authoritative; contribution history is only a confidence signal. Critical changes require primary and backup review. During an incident, page the primary first and the backup immediately for P1/security conditions. Jira transition history identifies who resolved a past issue, while catalog ownership identifies who is accountable now. BuildPulse shows both and never assumes they are the same person.
